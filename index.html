About us

Powering Zimbabwe's fields, mines and factories.

OMAK ENERGY ZW is a Zimbabwean company built to be the country's most reliable distributor of petroleum and gas products. We supply bulk and retail fuel to large and small-scale farmers, miners, manufacturers, corporates and the general public, helping maximise agricultural productivity while meeting global climate-action standards for conserving the environment.

Our short and long-term goal is to become a profitable, national independent service company with a fully developed technical services sector, engineering design capability and full operational presence across Zimbabwe, while investing in education, economic development and health as a responsible corporate citizen.
